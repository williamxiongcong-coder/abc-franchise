About the firm

We work with the founders we believe in.

Will&Bowen Franchise Consulting is a boutique advisory based in Sydney. Three active engagements at a time, every mandate led by a senior partner — no associates handed the file. This page is who we are, how we choose, and what we believe.

01 · Approach

A boutique, by design.

We take three engagements at a time. Not three a quarter — three at a time. The partner who hears your pitch is the partner who writes your strategy, opens your Disclosure Document, and sits across the table from your first franchisee.

We don't scale by hiring more associates. We scale by saying no — to the brands that aren't ready, the founders who aren't sure, and the timetables that aren't honest. The result is a slower pipeline and a sharper engagement.

02 · How we choose

Fit over volume.

We work with brands where the unit model is real. Where the founder has lived inside the business long enough to know what it's like when no one shows up at six in the morning. Where there is something specific worth protecting — not just a logo, but a way of doing the thing.

Two locations done deliberately mean more to us than ten done by accident. We're less interested in the size of your network today than in the integrity of what you've already built.

03 · What we believe

Four convictions that shape every mandate.

A franchise is a system, not a sale.

The moment the first franchisee signs, you become a software company that ships food, fitness, or beauty. The system has to scale — the closing pitch doesn't.

Compliance is the floor, not the ceiling.

The Franchising Code gives you a licence to operate. Trust — operational, contractual, cultural — is what gives you a network.

Speed is a symptom.

Brands that recruit fast usually recruit badly. Selectivity is the only edge worth pricing into a recruitment funnel.

The franchisor and franchisee are partners.

When they behave as adversaries, the brand fails twice — once at the unit, once in the system. Everything we draft is written from that premise.

04 · Where we sit

Sydney, with one foot in Asia.

We are based in Sydney. Most of our work is in New South Wales; some of it is national; a small and growing share runs across the Tasman and into Greater China and Southeast Asia.

We can read the Australian Code of Conduct and a Mandarin-language master franchise term sheet. That second skill is rare in this market, and it shapes the kind of brands we are most useful to.
