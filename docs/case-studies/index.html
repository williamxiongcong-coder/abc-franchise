The pattern

Three stages of one journey.

Lumina, Vitaland and Lermont sit at three different points of the franchise lifecycle — and that's deliberate. Three engagements at a time, ideally one in each stage, so the firm's expertise stays calibrated across the full arc.

Stage I — Build. The work is feasibility, model, and documentation. Lumina is here. The first franchisee is twelve months away.

Stage II — Scale. The work is recruitment discipline, playbook refinement, and franchisee onboarding. Vitaland is here. The next ten venues will be decided by intake quality, not market size.

Stage III — Capital. The work is investor structuring, intra-group licensing, and clinical site selection. Lermont is here. The 2 → 5 expansion is funded.

If your business sits at any of these stages — and you want partner-level attention, not associate-level templates — that's the conversation we'd like to have.
