The brand

Lumina is a specialty cafe brand based in Sydney's North-West, running on a deliberately simple promise: good coffee, good food, good people. Two venues operating — Macquarie Park and Castle Hill — with a third on the way in Rouse Hill. Warm golds, soft creams, latte art that gets photographed every morning.

Beneath the warmth is a discipline. The menu is short and seasonal. The team is trained the same way at every site. The brand was built to be replicable from the start — Benny just didn't have the franchise architecture to take it further on his own.

Where Benny came to us

With two venues operating, customer demand was outpacing capacity. Locals were asking when Rouse Hill would open. Property agents were calling about Eastwood, Chatswood, Parramatta. Benny had a choice: open the next stores himself, or build the system that allowed others to.

He chose the second.

The engagement

Lumina is inside the Strategic Consulting phase of the four-stage growth journey. The work, briefly:

  • A unit economics deep-dive across the two operating venues
  • The franchise model design — single-unit, NSW-first, with area developer rights reserved for scale partners
  • A five-year financial architecture taking Lumina from three units to sixteen
  • A brand-system audit ahead of the operations manual build

What's next

Once Strategic Consulting closes (six weeks in), we move into System Architecture (operations manual, training, supply, technology stack), then Legal & Compliance (the Disclosure Document, the Franchise Agreement), and then Recruitment — the first three to five franchisees, chosen deliberately rather than quickly.

If everything holds, the first Lumina franchisee signs in mid-2026.
