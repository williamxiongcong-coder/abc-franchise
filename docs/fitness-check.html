    Methodology

    How we score franchise readiness.

    The nine factors are drawn from twenty years of Australian franchise assessment work. Each factor is weighted by the prevalence with which it predicts year-two network failure, based on our own engagement data and Franchise Council of Australia industry reporting. Read the underlying framework in Criteria for Franchisability.

    This is a directional self-assessment, not a clinical audit. Two questions per factor cannot replace a structured feasibility study. Use the score to decide whether to go deeper — not as a green light to start franchising.
