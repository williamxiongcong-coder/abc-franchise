Why this brand

Premium tea + bakery — not generic bubble tea.

Multi-format network

Six distinct concept formats — Wonderland, Tea Lab, Go, Black, Aqua, Tea & Bakery. Each tuned to its catchment. The format is the moat: a competitor opens the same generic bubble tea store every time; Bengong's matches the venue to the format.

Tea + bakery, not tea alone

The bakery vertical — celebration cakes, custom orders, dessert-cabinet sales — adds a second revenue stream that pure tea shops don't have. Higher average ticket, less time-of-day concentration, real margin in the dessert category.

Central production at Holroyd

Production kitchen at 8-10 Fox Street, Holroyd — supplies all seven Sydney stores. Franchisees inherit a working supply chain on day one, not a "you'll figure out your suppliers" handoff.
