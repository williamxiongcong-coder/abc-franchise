How it works

From enquiry to opening, six steps.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Submit the form on the brand's opportunity page. A partner reviews within one business day.

  2. 02

    Discovery call

    A 30-minute conversation — about you, about the brand, about whether the fit is real.

  3. 03

    Information pack

    Detailed financials, territory map, Disclosure Document. Under NDA.

  4. 04

    Discovery day

    A day on the ground at an operating venue. The founder, the team, the reality.

  5. 05

    Agreement

    Independent legal review (we'll recommend a lawyer if you don't have one). 14-day cooling-off.

  6. 06

    Open

    Site selection, fit-out, training, and a senior team on the ground for opening week.
