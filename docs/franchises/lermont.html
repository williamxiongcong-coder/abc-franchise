About Lermont

Japanese precision, in the heart of Sydney.

Lermont operates two sister brands side-by-side in Sydney's Haymarket: Lermont Laser Clinic (anti-ageing, body contouring, laser), and Lermont Skincare (Japanese skin management, head spa). Both run on the same standard — Japanese precision, with the equipment and protocols to back it.

Ten years in, the practice has built a 1,700-plus client base, 20-plus pieces of professional equipment, and twelve treatment categories. Repeat rates and average treatment values place Lermont in the upper quartile of independent medical aesthetics in Australia.

The next chapter is a 2 → 5 clinic expansion inside twelve months. Lermont Chatswood opens in December 2026; clinics four and five are being scoped in parallel. Capital partners are being received by invitation only.

Why Lermont

Six things worth knowing.

A decade of clinical trust

Ten years operating, 1,700-plus clients, repeat rates that anchor the business model. This is not a startup — it's an institution scaling.

Twin-brand defensibility

Two complementary specialties under one parent — Laser Clinic + Skincare. Cross-referral, cross-pricing, cross-loyalty. Hard to replicate.

Japanese standard, Sydney market

Trained protocols, hand-picked equipment, and the precision that the Sydney CBD demographic has been waiting for. A brand built around standard, not discount.

Capital partners welcomed

The 2 → 5 expansion is a capital-led story, not a franchisee one. Investor partners join at the group level, not the unit level.

Operational scaffolding built

Operations manual, training pathway, intra-group licensing, and disclosure documentation — all drafted by ABC Franchise ahead of clinic three.

Confidentiality, by design

All enquiries are received by ABC Franchise and routed directly to the firm's partners. Materials are shared only after a preliminary conversation.
