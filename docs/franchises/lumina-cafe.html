About Lumina

The Sydney brunch specialist.

Lumina was built on a simple promise: good coffee, good food, good people. Two operating venues in Sydney's North-West — Macquarie Park and Castle Hill — have turned that promise into a daily ritual for thousands of local families, professionals, and weekend brunch-seekers.

Behind the warmth is a discipline. The menu is short and seasonal. The beans are roasted to a Lumina-defined profile. The team is trained the same way at every site. Customers don't just visit — they come back, three mornings a week.

A third venue opens in Rouse Hill, and now we're ready to bring the right partners in. Lumina is a brand built to be replicated — without losing what made the first stores work.

Why Lumina

Six reasons partners choose us.

A model that's already working

Two operating venues, profitable unit economics, repeatable across formats. You're not buying a prototype — you're buying a proven system.

Specialty coffee, not generic

A defined roast profile, training pathway and barista standards. Your store opens with the same cup as Macquarie Park, on day one.

A documented operations system

From open-shift checklist to seasonal menu rotation — every part of the day is written down, tested and trainable.

Site selection support

We've learnt where Lumina works (high-foot North-West Sydney precincts, family demographics) and where it doesn't. We won't waste your capital.

Operator-led training

Pre-opening immersion at an operating Lumina, plus an on-site opening team for the first 14 days. You're not figuring it out alone.

Australian Franchising Code compliant

Disclosure Document, Franchise Agreement and Information Statement all aligned to the current Code — drafted by ABC Franchise's legal practice.
