Why this brand

A category-defining brand, not a me-too tea concept.

Category dominance

NingJi is the №1 fresh lemon tea brand in China by store count and market share (21.6%). In 2024, 3 out of every 5 new lemon tea stores in China was a NingJi. Fastest brand in history to reach 2,000 stores — 3.5 years.

Proprietary supply

Owns 1,000+ acres of company-controlled fragrant lemon (香水柠檬) plantations in Guangdong / Guangxi. Six domestic distribution hubs and a Malaysia regional warehouse under construction. Stable cost. Stable supply. Stable margin for franchisees.

Blue-chip investors

Series A: ByteDance + Shunwei Capital (July 2021). Series A+: Tencent (January 2022). Existing shareholders oversubscribed. Capital depth means the franchisor side is well-resourced behind every market it enters.

The franchise package

Revenue-share model — not a traditional franchise fee.

NingJi runs a revenue-share partnership model for AU/NZ. No large upfront franchise fee — instead, an ongoing brand fee plus tiered management fee on revenue. The franchisor only earns when the store earns.
