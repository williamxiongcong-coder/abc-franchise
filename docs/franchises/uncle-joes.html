Why this brand

Real Malaysian — in a market that's never had it scaled.

Authentic, demonstrated

Two Sydney venues running at 4.3-star ratings, customer reviews repeatedly praising authenticity of laksa, curries, traditional dishes. This isn't a "fast-casual reinterpretation" — it's real Malaysian, scaled.

Sauce system as IP

The sauce system is the franchise IP. Centrally produced, recipe-protected, supplied to every venue. This solves the biggest scaling problem for ethnic cuisine — keeping flavour consistent without depending on chef tongue.

Whitespace markets

Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Cairns, Adelaide, Hobart — five regional markets with growing Malaysian-Australian populations and no scaled Malaysian restaurant brand. First-mover positioning.
