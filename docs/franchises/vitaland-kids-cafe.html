About Vitaland

The category-defining family cafe in Australia.

Vitaland combines an indoor playground with a real cafe — the kind of place where parents will actually choose to spend a Sunday morning. Train rides, ball pits, themed parties, and the kind of coffee that earns return visits beyond the play hour.

Eight venues today span NSW (Liverpool, Miranda, Alexandria, St Leonards, Warriewood, Eastwood, Rhodes) and Western Australia (City West, Perth). The model has been proven inside major shopping centres, as a stand-alone destination, and as a regional anchor.

Ten years in, with thirty thousand families through the door, the franchise programme is live. Vitaland is selecting partners deliberately — looking for operators who treat hospitality as a craft, not a side hustle.

Why Vitaland

Six reasons partners choose us.

A decade of validation

Ten-plus years of operating data — across multiple formats and two states. You're not betting on a concept; you're buying into a model that's already proven.

Dual revenue streams

Cafe revenue plus play-session fees plus the high-margin parties business. Resilient against any single channel having a soft week.

Built-in repeat customers

Memberships, parties, and parent-coffee-while-kid-plays make Vitaland a weekly habit — not a once-off destination.

Site selection from real data

Eight venues' worth of foot-traffic data tells us what works (and what doesn't). We won't sign a lease that won't hold.

Hands-on opening team

A senior team on the ground for opening week, immersion at an operating venue beforehand, and a 90-day check-in cadence after.

Code-of-Conduct compliant
