Confidentiality Agreement — Terms

1. Parties. This agreement is between the person named in the form below ("you") and ABC Franchise Pty Ltd, acting for itself and on behalf of the franchisor of the Lumina Cafe brand (together, "we", "us").

2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means all information we disclose to you in connection with a possible Lumina Cafe franchise, including financial performance data, unit economics, supplier terms, operations materials, territory plans, the Disclosure Document and any other information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential. It does not include information that is public through no fault of yours, or that you can show you already lawfully held.

3. Purpose. You may use Confidential Information solely to evaluate whether to proceed with a Lumina Cafe franchise (the "Purpose"), and for no other purpose — including any competing or similar business.

4. Non-disclosure. You must keep Confidential Information confidential and not disclose it to anyone except your professional advisers (lawyer, accountant, financier) who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by obligations of confidence, or as required by law.

5. Return or destruction. If you do not proceed, or if we ask at any time, you must promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and copies, and confirm in writing that you have done so.

6. No offer; no obligation. Providing information to you is not an offer of a franchise, and neither party is obliged to proceed. Any grant of a franchise will follow the disclosure and cooling-off process required by the Franchising Code of Conduct.

7. Duration. Your obligations under this agreement continue for three (3) years from the date of your acceptance below.

8. Remedies. You acknowledge that damages may be an inadequate remedy for breach, and that we may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other rights.

9. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia, and the parties submit to the jurisdiction of its courts.

10. Electronic acceptance. You agree that completing and submitting this form, including typing your full legal name below, constitutes your acceptance of this agreement and has the same effect as a handwritten signature.

Your details are handled under the Australian Privacy Act and shared only with the Lumina Cafe franchisor. Questions first? admin@abcfranchise.com.au
